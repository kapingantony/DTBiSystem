Innovation ecosystem · Tanzania

From community learning to enterprise growth

BUNI Innovation Hub and Dar Teknohama Business Incubator have supported technology, entrepreneurship, and innovation in Tanzania since 2011. This history page preserves the context behind the programmes while the impact explorer reports operational records entered into this system.

2011 · ESTABLISHMENT

Two complementary hubs

The study describes DTBi as established by COSTECH with World Bank infoDev collaboration to nurture ICT enterprises. Buni began as the TANZICT Innovation Space under a Tanzania–Finland cooperation project.

2013 · COMMUNITY

Buni Innovation Hub

The name Buni Innovation Hub came into use as the programme focused on building local innovation capacity and connecting young people to technology entrepreneurship.

2014 ONWARD · MAKING

From ideas to prototypes

The study records Buni programmes spanning internships, mentoring, makerspace access, and community outreach. It describes the makerspace as a place for rapid prototyping and fabrication.

2011–2020 · ECOSYSTEM

Connecting ventures and partners

Documented work includes startup incubation, skills building, access to networks, collaboration with public institutions, and support for other innovation hubs and community initiatives.

Programme pathways described in the study

Archived examples · not open calls

Internship and skills building

Team based learning and practical projects helped participants develop problem solving, collaboration, design, prototyping, and pitching skills.

Mentoring and pre-incubation

Participants with ideas and early products could develop business models and minimum viable products, then seek further incubation support.

Makerspace and prototyping

Fabrication and rapid prototyping enabled hands on exploration of hardware, 3D printing, drones, and connected devices.

Community outreach

Outreach connected universities and community networks with innovation, entrepreneurship, and technology activities.
